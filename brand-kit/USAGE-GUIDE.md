# Kaasth · काष्ठ: Logo usage guide
Designed by BSX Agency · Concept demo brand (fictional)

## The mark
**Jharokha Seal.** A Devanagari **क** (ka, for काष्ठ, "wood") sits inside a Rajasthani jharokha window arch.
The letter's headline line doubles as the window's crossbar. Tagline: *Made to be inherited.*

## Colours
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walnut | `#3A2418` | 58, 36, 24 | Logo, headings, dark sections |
| Brass | `#B08D57` | 176, 141, 87 | "Est." line, accents, thin rules (not for small body text) |
| Ivory | `#F3ECE0` | 243, 236, 224 | Backgrounds, logo on walnut |

Walnut on Ivory has very high contrast (about 12.4:1). Brass on Ivory is about 2.6:1, so use Brass only for decorative text on Ivory. On Walnut it reaches 4.7:1 and is fine for text.

## Type
- **Cormorant Garamond** (SemiBold, wide letter-spacing) for the wordmark and display. SIL Open Font License.
- **Tiro Devanagari Hindi** for the क and Hindi accents. SIL Open Font License.
- The logo letters are outlined shapes, so no font files are needed for the logo itself.

## Rules
- **Clear space:** keep a gap around the logo at least as wide as the arch's side wall × 2 (about ⅛ of the symbol's height).
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px (use the small cut below 32 px); horizontal lockup 120 px wide; the "Est." line can be dropped below 160 px.
- **Approved backgrounds:** Ivory, white, Walnut (reversed version), or calm wood or stone photos with enough contrast.
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate or outline the mark; recolour the क separately from the arch; add shadows or gradients; put it on busy photos; rewrite "KAASTH" in another font.

# Kaasth · काष्ठ: Brief (Stage 1, approved 2026-11-24)
*Concept demo by BSX Agency, shown to furniture business owners when pitching. Designed & built by BSX Agency.*

- **Brand:** Kaasth (काष्ठ, "wood"). Tagline *Made to be inherited.* Fictional family workshop, Ahmedabad, est. 1962.
- **Collections:** Heirloom · विरासत (restored, hand-carved antiques) and Form · रूप (modern solid wood).
- **Audience:** Indian homeowners furnishing or upgrading a home. Fears: veneer sold as solid wood, termites/borers, bad delivery and after-sales, fake antiques.
- **Positioning:** every piece carries a **Wood Passport** (wood, joinery, termite treatment, artisan hours, warranty); Heirloom pieces add a provenance card.
- **Actions (in order):** 1. Book a showroom visit (date + slot) · 2. Enquire about a piece (form or WhatsApp) · 3. General contact.
- **Market:** India, ₹ prices ("From ₹48,000"; antiques "Price on request"). English + Hinglish lines + Devanagari display words.
- **Pages:** Home, Products (+ detail pages), Services, About, Contact, Privacy, 404.
- **Reviews:** sample reviews allowed; a "Concept demo by BSX Agency" badge stays visible site-wide.
- **Re-skin:** brand, colours, contact and the whole catalogue live in one config file so BSX can adapt the demo for a prospect.
- **No backend:** forms validate and show success (demo mode); a live mode can be switched on per client.
- **Photos:** product shots made by the owner in Gemini/Google Flow from our prompts; rooms/workshop from Pexels/Unsplash; hero = ThreeRT renders. Nothing paid without asking.
- **3D:** two moments: the home hero room (Heirloom → Form) and a live rotating chair on its product page.
